I’m a software engineer based in Adelaide, South Australia, with a Bachelor of Mathematical and Computer Sciences from the University of Adelaide. I'm interested in the intersection of mathematics, machine learning, and systems programming. 

I care more about understanding why something works than just getting it to run. Most of my personal projects are built from first principles rather than off-the-shelf libraries. I’ve implemented a multilayer perceptron and a convolutional neural network from scratch in NumPy, deriving backpropagation, gradient checking, and loss functions by hand. I’m currently building a 3D rigid-body physics engine from scratch in C++, including my own math library, as a long-term project to deepen my systems and numerical programming skills.

I also built Orbit, a graph-based Steam social exploration tool using the Steam API, with caching and incremental graph expansion, deployed with Flask and Gunicorn.

Beyond code, I completed an ANU China Studies Fellowship, spending a year studying in China and reaching HSK5-level Mandarin proficiency. I also worked as an Embedded Software Engineer intern at Silentium Defence. Outside of technical work, I read widely in philosophy and literature.
