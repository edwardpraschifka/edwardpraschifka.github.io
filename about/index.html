About

I'm a graduate software engineer based in Adelaide, South Australia, with a Bachelor of Mathematical and Computer Sciences from the University of Adelaide. My work sits at the intersection of mathematics, machine learning, and systems programming, across embedded and backend development.

I care more about understanding why something works than just getting it to run. Most of my personal projects are built from first principles rather than off-the-shelf libraries. I've implemented a multilayer perceptron and a convolutional neural network from scratch in NumPy, deriving backpropagation, gradient checking, and loss functions by hand. I'm currently building a 3D rigid-body physics engine from scratch in C++, including my own math library, as a long-term project to deepen my systems and numerical programming skills.

I also built Orbit, a graph-based Steam social exploration tool using the Steam API, with caching and incremental graph expansion, backed by Flask and deployed on Render.

Beyond code, I completed an ANU China Studies Fellowship, spending a year studying in China and reaching HSK5 (Professional Working Proficiency) Mandarin. I also worked as an Embedded Software Engineer intern at Silentium Defence. Outside of technical work, I read widely in philosophy and literature.

I'm now looking for graduate roles in software development and embedded software development, particularly where I can apply my Mandarin proficiency and China Studies background in China-facing or defence-adjacent work.

I'm currently looking for a Graduate Software Developer Role! You can contact me on my linkedin or by email at edwardpraschifka@gmail.com.
